Enterprise Compliance & Harassment Safeguards

We acknowledge that relationship features inside a company present significant compliance responsibilities. Our system mitigates risks cleanly through state-of-the-art technical boundaries.

1
Global Admin Switch: HR managers can turn the discovery layer on/off completely or limit it exclusively to "Friendship & Professional Networking".
2
Strict Double Opt-In: No dating or relationship status is public. It is only unlocked to a target user if BOTH users explicitly indicate interest.
3
Reporting Safeguards: Embedded reporting capabilities linked to HR compliance tools to deter unwanted behaviors.
